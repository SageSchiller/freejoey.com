An ongoing, extremely informal log of things that happened, things we are choosing to call "evidence," and updates on the campaign. Not a court record. Not a deposition. Please stop citing this in your defense.

[09-15-1995 22:14 EST] LOG 001

FIRST CONTACT

Somebody uploaded a section of proprietary code to a garbage file. It sat there. Untouched. Waiting for nobody in particular. Then a bored teenager decided to find out what happens when you poke a system you don't recognize the size of. That's the whole origin story. Not exactly a criminal mastermind moment, and yet here we are.

[09-16-1995 03:02 EST] LOG 002

THE FALLOUT

They came through the door before sunrise. Enough agents for a bedroom that did not require them. They left with the floppies, with the machine he called Lucy, and with the kid, in roughly that order of apparent priority.

Nobody has explained what they expected to find, or why a "worm" that no one in the local scene wrote required all of this. The explanation would have to come from someone willing to give one.

[10-01-1995 14:00 EST] LOG 003

WHERE HE STANDS NOW

In custody. No charges announced. No release date given. The agency that took him has not explained what it believes a teenager contributed to a multimillion dollar embezzlement scheme, and has not been asked to.

Meanwhile the disk he hid is the only reason anyone can prove the scheme existed at all. Sit with that ordering of events for a moment.

[10-04-1995 02:55 EST] LOG 004

THE AGENT

The raid was run by Special Agent Richard Gill of the United States Secret Service. He led it personally. He has given no interviews, filed no public statement, and named no charge.

He is also, by his own reported account, a man who does not especially understand what he seized, which has not slowed him down even slightly.

The record notes that within days of the raid, Agent Gill's credit rating collapsed, a personal advertisement appeared in his name in several papers, and at least one government database briefly listed him as deceased. The campaign was not consulted about any of this and has no information regarding who was.

[10-04-1995 03:10 EST] LOG 005

A CALL TO ACTION

So: hack the man's life. Ruin his credit. Sign him up for things. Put him in the classifieds. Make the next thirty years of his paperwork an ordeal. He took a kid's computer out of a bedroom before sunrise and he can live with the consequences.

This campaign has no capability to do any of that, has never had any, and would not know where to begin. Our entire technical apparatus is one BBS with a single node and a website nobody has updated since the Clinton administration. Consider the above a statement of feeling.

Agent Gill is a fictional character in a 1995 film. Everything described in LOG 004 happens to him on screen, at the hands of people considerably more competent than us. Do not do any of it to anybody real.

[03-14-2026 09:41 EST] LOG 006

THIRTY-ONE YEARS LATER

This site went up in 1995 and nobody ever took it down. The campaign continues, in the sense that a campaign with no leadership, no funding, and no legal standing can be said to "continue." Somebody still pays for the domain. Nobody remembers who.
