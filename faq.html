1. Is Joey a real person?

No. Joey Pardella is a fictional character from the 1995 film Hackers, played by Jesse Bradford. He is raided and taken into custody by the Secret Service in the film. He is not in custody in real life, on account of not being a real person.

2. Is this a real fundraiser?

No. Nothing on this site takes money or collects anything from you. The donate button is a gag and always has been.

3. Why does this site exist?

You know the format. Somebody always ends up on the stickers. We just think the pick was wrong, and some of us identify a little too hard with the least confident guy in the room.

4. Are you affiliated with the movie, MGM, or United Artists?

No. This is an unofficial fan project made purely for fun and commentary. All rights to Hackers (1995) belong to their respective owners.

5. Can I submit evidence or updates?

There's no contact form, on purpose. Feel free to yell into the void instead. It has historically been just as effective.

6. Why does this website look like this?

Because 1995 had the aesthetic right the first time, and nobody has meaningfully improved on Program Manager since.
