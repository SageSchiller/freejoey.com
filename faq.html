1. Is Joey a real person?

No. Joey Pardella is a fictional character from the 1995 film Hackers, played by Jesse Bradford. Nobody is being held anywhere, freed, or otherwise legally affected by this website.

2. Is this a real fundraiser?

No. We do not collect money, personal information, or payment details anywhere on this site. If anything on this site ever asks you for a card number, close the tab immediately and assume the worst.

3. Why does this site exist?

Because "Free Kevin" was a real hacker solidarity campaign in the 1990s protesting Kevin Mitnick's imprisonment, and we thought Joey deserved a little of that energy too — even though, again, nobody arrested him. Some of us just identify with the not-quite-confident-enough hacker in the group chat.

4. Are you affiliated with the movie, MGM, or United Artists?

No. This is an unofficial fan project made purely for fun and commentary. All rights to Hackers (1995) belong to their respective owners. This site claims no ownership over the film, its characters, or its aesthetic beyond obvious parody.

5. Can I submit evidence or updates?

There's no contact form, on purpose — see question 2 about not collecting anything from you. Feel free to yell into the void instead. It has historically been just as effective.

6. Why does this website look like this?

Because 1995 had the aesthetic right the first time, and nobody has meaningfully improved on Program Manager since.

7. What happened to Kevin, the guy this is based on?

Kevin Mitnick was a real person, released from federal custody in 2000, and later became a well-known security consultant and author before his death in 2023. His story is real and well documented; Joey's is not. Please don't confuse the two, and please don't send Kevin's family messages intended for a fictional character.
